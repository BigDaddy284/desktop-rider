# SleepEnd

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

The rider wakes up. Leads back to Idle. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 0.3 s | Head jerks up. | Fork extends as the weight comes off the bars. |
| 2 | 0.3 to 0.8 s | Sits upright, shoulders back, a quick look left and right. | Rocks slightly. |
| 3 | 0.8 to 1.1 s | Hands settle on the grips, back to the resting pose. | Still. |

Waking with a start is quicker than falling asleep: the head comes up first and the body follows.

## What to change for the 3D version

v1 is close to SleepStart played backwards. In 3D make the head lead and add a small shake or look around.
