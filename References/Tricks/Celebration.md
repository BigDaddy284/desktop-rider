# Celebration

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

A victory gesture. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 0.4 s | Sits up tall or stands on the pegs, one hand leaves the bar. | Still, or rolling slowly. |
| 2 | 0.4 to 1.4 s | Fist punched straight up above the helmet, held, sometimes pumped twice. Chest lifted, head up. | Rocks back slightly on the rear suspension as the weight shifts. |
| 3 | 1.4 to 2.1 s | Arm comes down, hand back on the grip. | Settles. |

The classic finish-line celebrations are a one-handed fist pump standing on the pegs, both arms out wide (a no hander over the finish jump), or a small whip over the finish. Racers normally keep the right hand on the throttle and raise the left.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. From the original base animation set.
- **Length:** 25 sprite frames, 2.1 s.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Seated rider punches the near fist straight up above the helmet and brings it down. Feet stay on the pegs.

## What to change for the 3D version

Works. In 3D raise the left fist so the throttle hand stays on, and consider standing on the pegs for a bigger shape.
