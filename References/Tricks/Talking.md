# Talking

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

Shown while the rider's reply is being displayed. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Loop | Helmet nods and tips in small movements, one or two a second, in time with speech. A hand may lift from the bar to gesture. | Still. |

The mouth cannot be seen behind the chin bar, so talking has to be shown with the head and hands. People in helmets exaggerate nods and hand gestures for exactly this reason.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. From the original base animation set.
- **Length:** 12 sprite frames, a 1.0 s loop.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Small repeated head movement while seated with both hands on the bars.

## What to change for the 3D version

A 1 s loop repeats very visibly over a long reply. For 3D drive the nods from the text as it appears, and mix in an occasional hand gesture.
