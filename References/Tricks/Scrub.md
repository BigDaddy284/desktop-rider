# Scrub

**Kind:** Motocross move

## What it is

A racing technique for staying low. The bike is leaned hard over on the face of the jump so it leaves the lip sideways and low instead of upright and high, then it is picked up before landing. Made famous by James Stewart, so often called the Bubba scrub.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | A little faster than normal. Brakes firmly to compress the suspension, then absorbs the face with arms and legs. | Suspension loaded low. |
| 2 | Up the face | Turns slightly across the face and pushes the bike down sideways underneath. Body weight goes to the opposite side to balance. | Leaning further and further while both tyres still grip. If either slides, it is a crash. |
| 3 | Lip | Inside knee pressed hard into the bike. The outside foot may come off its peg with that knee tucked up under the elbow. Bars turned toward the lean. | Already well laid over as it leaves the ground. Leaves flatter and lower than a normal jump. |
| 4 | In the air | Low over the bike, looking at the landing. | On its side, low, travelling almost straight. Spends less time in the air than a normal jump. |
| 5 | Recovery | Pulls the bike upright under the body. | Back to vertical before the wheels touch. |
| 6 | Landing | Centred, straight onto the throttle or the brakes, never coasting. | Upright, often already pointing toward the next corner. |

The whole difference from a whip is timing: a scrub is done on the face and at the lip to stay low, a whip is done after the lip for style.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** low dirt take-off 24°, lip 0.42 m. Landing mound 0.72 m, 22° down slope.
- **Timeline (seconds):** leaves the lip at 1.67, in the air for only 1.04 (the Jump has 1.33), lands at 2.71, brakes from 3.58, stopped at 4.58. 56 sprite frames. Peak height 1.49 m against 1.87 m for the Jump.
- **Scrub timing after take-off:** about 15% leaned at the moment it leaves, full at 0.17 s, held to 0.35 s, upright by 0.81 s.
- **Bike at full scrub:** rolled 70° toward the camera, yawed 24°, bars turned 26°. The nose starts turning down immediately, with no hold.
- **Rider:** low, hips shifted 6 cm, chest tilted 18° and head 26° against the lean.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Low, flat flight with the bike on its side showing its top to the viewer, picked up just before the landing.

## What to change for the 3D version

Right idea, wrong start. In v1 the bike is still upright on the face and the lean arrives in the first 0.17 s of flight. In a real scrub most of the lean is already there on the face before the wheels leave. In 3D begin the lean halfway up the take-off and have the bike leave the lip about two thirds leaned.

## Best views

Rear view straight up the take-off shows the sweep across the face and the lean. Side-on shows how low it stays compared with a normal jump.
