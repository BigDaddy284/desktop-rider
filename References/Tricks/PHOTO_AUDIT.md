# Photograph audit: which views are missing

Version 2 shows the rider and bike from every side, so each item needs to be known from the front, back, both sides and above. This lists what the owner's reference photographs in `References/Original-Photographs/` cover and what they do not: 28 from the first three batches and 59 action photographs plus 2 videos added on 13 November 2026.

The photographs themselves were only viewed. None was changed, moved or copied. They are kept out of this public repository, and personal details are left out of this file in the same way as in the visual reference summary.

Audited on 13 November 2026 and updated the same day for the action set.

The owner said on 12 November 2026 that no further photographs exist of the rear of the bike or of the gloves. Those two need a decision on how to fill the gap, not another request.

## Found in the action set (13 November 2026)

The three action photographs in the correct kit show four things the earlier photographs did not. Each needs the owner's decision before it goes into the model, because CLAUDE.md does not allow guessing.

1. **Gloves are sky blue with pink and black** when riding. The visual reference summary records black gloves with white detailing from the standing photographs. Either there are two pairs or the earlier note is wrong.
2. **A black chest protector with shoulder cups is worn over the jersey** when riding. It hides most of the jersey front and the chest logo. The 3D rider currently has no chest protector.
3. **The front number plate reads B28**: a class letter B sits before the 28.
4. **The top of the helmet** is visible: sky blue, yellow and pink stripes radiating back from the peak, with a pink edge to the peak.

The bike in these photographs matches the later state already recorded (pink rims, pink ignition cover).

The 56 older-kit photographs and both videos are for posture and movement only. See [RIDER_STYLE.md](RIDER_STYLE.md) for what they show.

## Notes for the 3D model

- **Left and right are not mirror images** on the bike, the boots or the helmet. Do not mirror textures to fill a missing side.
- **Helmet right side** is the largest unknown on the rider. The front and back photographs show that the pattern is not symmetrical.
- **Trouser sides** carry the brand lettering down the outer thigh on most kits of this type. The photographs do not show whether this kit has it.
- **No photograph shows the rider standing on the pegs from the side in the correct kit.** The older-kit photographs cover the pose itself.
- Two phone videos now exist (a start and a distant lap). Neither shows the correct kit.
