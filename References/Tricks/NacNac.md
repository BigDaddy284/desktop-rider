# NacNac

**Kind:** Freestyle trick

## What it is

One leg is swung backwards over the rear mudguard to the other side of the bike, as if getting off, while the rider looks back. Brought from BMX to motocross by Jeremy McGrath in 1994. The name is 'can can' backwards.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On the ramp | Arcs slightly up the face to start a whip. | Leaning a little. |
| 2 | Lip to 20% | Stands on the peg on the side the bike is whipped toward. | The rear swings out sideways, away from the leg that will come off. |
| 3 | 20% to 40% | The outside foot leaves its peg and the straight leg swings back and around over the rear mudguard. | Whipped: back end out to one side, bike laid partly over. |
| 4 | 40% to 60% | Both legs on one side, the swung leg stretched out behind and away from the bike. Hips turned, head looking back over the shoulder. | Still whipped. |
| 5 | 60% to 85% | Leg swings back over the mudguard to its peg while the bike straightens. | Back end comes back into line. |
| 6 | Landing | Standing. | Straight or nearly straight, rear first. |

The whip is part of the trick: turning the bike sideways under the rider is what makes room to swing the leg over the back. On a bike flying dead straight the leg has to be lifted much higher.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** steel freestyle ramp, 38° at the lip, lip 0.5 m high, landing on a dirt mound 0.8 m high with a 31° down slope. Bike speed 3 m/s. Gravity is scaled to 2.6 m/s² so a small ramp gives real-looking hang time.
- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, leaves the lip at 1.46, in the air for 1.79, lands at 3.25, brakes from 4.04, stopped at 5.08. That is 122 keyed frames at 24 a second, 62 sprite frames at 12 a second.
- **Height:** the centre of bike and rider peaks about 2.4 m above the ground.
- **Bike in the air:** 38° nose up at the lip, held for the first 0.27 s, then eases over to 27° nose down just before landing. No roll, no yaw, bars straight. Rear wheel spins 15% faster than on the ground, front wheel slows to half. Suspension hangs out in the air (30 mm rear, 45 mm fork), compresses on the ramp face and on landing, with one small overshoot after the hit.
- **Rider on the ground:** seated to pull away, up into the attack position by 0.7 s, legs soak the ramp, a small pop at the lip, legs soak the landing, sits back to rest while braking.
- **Trick timing after take-off:** starts moving at 0.25 s, fully in the pose at 0.68 s, held until 1.07 s, back in the riding position by 1.50 s. That leaves 0.29 s on the bike before the wheels touch.
- **Pose in the code:** the near foot stays on its peg. The far foot goes 0.85 m behind the pegs, 0.42 m out on the camera's side and 0.72 m high. Hips turn 30°, chest a further 18° and head 48° toward the camera. The bike flies straight, with no roll or yaw.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Standing on the near peg, twisted to face the viewer, with the far leg behind the bike over the rear mudguard.

## What to change for the 3D version

The rider's pose is right but the bike is wrong: v1 flies straight, where the real trick is done on a whipped bike. For 3D add a moderate whip (roughly half of the Whip sheet's roll and yaw) timed with the leg. As with CanCan, fix which leg does it.

## Best views

Rear three-quarter from the side the leg swings to. In McGrath's version the right leg swings over and he hangs off the left side of the bike.
