# BarHop

**Kind:** Freestyle trick

## What it is

Both feet come up between the arms, over the bars and out in front, then back through to the pegs before landing
(Red Bull's FMX trick list: the rider "puts both feet through the handlebars then brings them back through"). Landing
with the feet still through the bars is a Sterilizer.

## In Desktop Rider

Until 14 November 2026 this animation was called HeelClicker. Boss pointed out that the legs pass between the arms, so
it is a bar hop; it was renamed and a new HeelClicker made. The pose is the same: hips back, feet 0.8 m ahead of the
pegs at 1.2 m, close together.
