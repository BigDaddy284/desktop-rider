# LookLeft

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

The rider turns the head to look to their left. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 0.4 s | Eyes and helmet start to turn left. The helmet peak makes even a small turn easy to read. | Still. |
| 2 | 0.4 to 1.0 s | Head turned about 60° to 70°, shoulders rotated 10° to 20° the same way. Held. | Still. Bars stay straight. |
| 3 | 1.0 to 1.4 s | Returns to face forward. | Still. |

A helmeted rider cannot turn the head as far as a bare one: the chin bar meets the shoulder and chest protector at around 70°. To look further, the shoulders turn too.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. From the original base animation set.
- **Length:** 17 sprite frames, 1.4 s.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

The helmet turns to the rider's left and back. Seen side-on, one direction shows the face opening and the other shows the back of the helmet.

## What to change for the 3D version

In v1, left and right were separate drawings because the camera was fixed. In 3D one head-turn with a target angle covers both, and the rider can look at the mouse pointer or the chat bubble instead.
