# IndianAir

**Kind:** Freestyle trick

## What it is

A superman seat grab in which the legs cross over each other in a scissor at full stretch. Taken from BMX. In competition it is normally done with the seat grab, not with both hands on the bars.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lip to 30% | Exactly as a superman seat grab: feet off, body back, one hand to the seat. | Nose up, front slightly light. |
| 2 | 30% to 45% | At full stretch one leg goes up and the other down, crossing at the knees or shins. | Level. |
| 3 | 45% to 60% | The crossed position is held, or the legs swap once so the other leg is on top. One or two clean crosses, not a flutter. | Level. |
| 4 | 60% to 85% | Legs uncross and come together, then the rider pulls back in as from a seat grab. | Nose drops. |
| 5 | Landing | Standing, both hands on. | Rear slightly first. |

Same bike behaviour as the seat grab. The scissor itself hardly moves the bike because the legs move in opposite directions.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** steel freestyle ramp, 38° at the lip, lip 0.5 m high, landing on a dirt mound 0.8 m high with a 31° down slope. Bike speed 3 m/s. Gravity is scaled to 2.6 m/s² so a small ramp gives real-looking hang time.
- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, leaves the lip at 1.46, in the air for 1.79, lands at 3.25, brakes from 4.04, stopped at 5.08. That is 122 keyed frames at 24 a second, 62 sprite frames at 12 a second.
- **Height:** the centre of bike and rider peaks about 2.4 m above the ground.
- **Bike in the air:** 38° nose up at the lip, held for the first 0.27 s, then eases over to 27° nose down just before landing. No roll, no yaw, bars straight. Rear wheel spins 15% faster than on the ground, front wheel slows to half. Suspension hangs out in the air (30 mm rear, 45 mm fork), compresses on the ramp face and on landing, with one small overshoot after the hit.
- **Rider on the ground:** seated to pull away, up into the attack position by 0.7 s, legs soak the ramp, a small pop at the lip, legs soak the landing, sits back to rest while braking.
- **Trick timing after take-off:** starts moving at 0.25 s, fully in the pose at 0.68 s, held until 1.07 s, back in the riding position by 1.50 s. That leaves 0.29 s on the bike before the wheels touch.
- **Pose in the code:** the plain Superman pose with both hands on the bars. The feet move up and down in opposite directions by 0.22 m, about three full scissors during the flight.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Looks like Superman with the two boots apart, one above the other. From the side the crossing is hard to read.

## What to change for the 3D version

Two differences from the real trick. First, the real one is done from a seat grab. Second, the real scissor is one or two deliberate crosses that are held, where v1 flutters about three times. In 3D the legs should also cross sideways (one over the other), which v1 could not show side-on.

## Best views

A rear or top three-quarter view is the only one that shows the legs crossing. Side-on hides it.
