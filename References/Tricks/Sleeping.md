# Sleeping

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

The sleeping loop, used when the user has been away. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Breathing in (about 2 s) | Slumped over the bars. Back rises slowly, helmet lifts a few millimetres. | Still. Engine off. |
| 2 | Breathing out (about 2 s) | Back sinks, helmet settles. | Still. |

Sleeping breathing is slow and deep, roughly one breath every 4 to 5 seconds, slower than the Idle breathing.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. From the original base animation set.
- **Length:** 48 sprite frames, a 4.0 s loop. Entered through SleepStart and left through SleepEnd.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Rider folded forward over the bars with the helmet down, rising and falling slowly.

## What to change for the 3D version

Works. With the engine off there should be no tick-over shiver. A resting stance with a foot on the ground matters most here, because a sleeping rider balancing a bike looks strangest of all.

## Best views

Side-on, or a front three-quarter from slightly above.
