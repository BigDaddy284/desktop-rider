# RockSolid

**Kind:** Freestyle trick

## What it is

From a superman seat grab the rider lets go completely, floats above the bike with nothing touching it, then catches it again and gets back on. One of the classic 'body varial' tricks.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lip to 30% | Into a seat grab, usually with both hands going back to the grab holes (a double grab). | Nose up, front light. |
| 2 | 30% to 40% | At full stretch, pushes off lightly and opens both hands. Arms spread out to the sides or straight ahead. | Carries on along its own path. It must already be flying straight and level, because nobody is steering. |
| 3 | 40% to 55% (the release) | Completely free: body flat above the back of the bike, arms out, legs straight. Rider and bike fall together so the gap stays small. | Untouched. Bars may flop a few degrees. |
| 4 | 55% to 65% | Hands come back down onto the seat or grab holes. | A small dip when the weight comes back on. |
| 5 | 65% to 88% | Pulls forward, hands to the grips, feet to the pegs. This trick leaves the least time to get back on. | Nose drops for the landing. |
| 6 | Landing | Often still settling onto the pegs. | Rear first, deep compression. |

The release only lasts a fraction of a second. The rider does not rise away from the bike: both are in free fall, so the hands stay within reach.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** steel freestyle ramp, 38° at the lip, lip 0.5 m high, landing on a dirt mound 0.8 m high with a 31° down slope. Bike speed 3 m/s. Gravity is scaled to 2.6 m/s² so a small ramp gives real-looking hang time.
- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, leaves the lip at 1.46, in the air for 1.79, lands at 3.25, brakes from 4.04, stopped at 5.08. That is 122 keyed frames at 24 a second, 62 sprite frames at 12 a second.
- **Height:** the centre of bike and rider peaks about 2.4 m above the ground.
- **Bike in the air:** 38° nose up at the lip, held for the first 0.27 s, then eases over to 27° nose down just before landing. No roll, no yaw, bars straight. Rear wheel spins 15% faster than on the ground, front wheel slows to half. Suspension hangs out in the air (30 mm rear, 45 mm fork), compresses on the ramp face and on landing, with one small overshoot after the hit.
- **Rider on the ground:** seated to pull away, up into the attack position by 0.7 s, legs soak the ramp, a small pop at the lip, legs soak the landing, sits back to rest while braking.
- **Trick timing after take-off:** starts moving at 0.25 s, fully in the pose at 0.68 s, held until 1.07 s, back in the riding position by 1.50 s. That leaves 0.29 s on the bike before the wheels touch.
- **Release in the code:** starts 0.64 s after take-off, completely free from 0.79 s to 1.00 s, hands back on by 1.15 s. During the release the hips lift another 5 cm, the body straightens out of its twist, the near hand moves out to the side behind and the far hand out to the side ahead.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Flat body above the rear of the bike with a clear gap between hands and bike for about three sprite frames.

## What to change for the 3D version

Good match and the timing is believable. v1 goes in with one hand on the seat and one on the bar; the real trick is normally entered from a double grab (both hands at the back). In 3D let the bars move slightly while released.
