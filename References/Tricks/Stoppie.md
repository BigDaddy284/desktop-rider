# Stoppie

**Kind:** Ground trick

## What it is

Also called a nose wheelie or endo. Hard front brake lifts the rear wheel off the ground, the rider balances on the front wheel, then sets the rear back down.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | Moderate speed in a straight line, standing or seated, weight moving forward over the bars. | Level. |
| 2 | Brake on | Squeezes the front brake smoothly but quickly. Weight forward. | Fork dives to the bottom of its travel. Rear suspension extends. |
| 3 | Rear lifts | Keeps squeezing. As the rear comes up, the arms straighten and the hips move back to control how high it goes. | Pivots around the front axle. Rear wheel off the ground and slowing or stopped. Still rolling forward on the front tyre. |
| 4 | Balance | Arms braced, knees gripping, small changes in brake pressure hold the angle. | Held at 30° to 45° on a dirt bike. Past the balance point it goes over the bars. |
| 5 | Coming down | Eases the brake lever. | Rear drops, lands, rear suspension compresses and settles. Fork extends. |

The lift comes from the brake and the weight shift together. Releasing the front brake is always the way out.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, front brake at 0.95, rear rising from 1.07, at full height by 1.55, balanced until 2.05, back down at 2.5, settled at 3.0. 37 sprite frames.
- **Bike:** speed drops by 82% in the first half second of braking. Fork dives 105 mm, rear extends 35 mm. Peak angle 40° nose down with small balancing corrections. The rear wheel stops turning while it is in the air. On landing the rear compresses 70 mm with one rebound.
- **Rider:** sits forward to start, then arms braced, hips 10 to 14 cm back and up to 13 cm up, leaning back against the pitch, head lifted to look ahead.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Bike up on its front wheel at about 40° with the rider standing tall and leaning back, then set down.

## What to change for the 3D version

Good match and good suspension detail. Real riders are forward over the bars at the instant of braking and only move back as the rear rises; v1 moves back straight away.

## Best views

Side-on shows the angle and fork dive. Rear three-quarter shows the rear wheel in the air.
