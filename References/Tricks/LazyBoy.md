# LazyBoy

**Kind:** Freestyle trick

## What it is

The feet go forward under the bars, the rider lies flat on their back along the seat and lets go, stretching the arms back over the head. With the hands kept on the bars it is called a coffin. Named after the reclining chair.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lip to 15% | Sits down straight away. | Nose up. |
| 2 | 15% to 40% | Feet leave the pegs and slide forward under the handlebar, knees hooked under the bar. The rider leans back. | Front rises a little as the weight goes back. |
| 3 | 40% to 58% | Flat on the back along the seat and rear mudguard, legs straight out in front, hands released and arms stretched back past the helmet. | Level. The legs under the bars are all that hold the rider on. |
| 4 | 58% to 84% | Sits up with a hard stomach crunch, hands to the grips, feet back and down to the pegs. | Nose drops. |
| 5 | Landing | Usually seated or only just standing. | Rear slightly first. |

The sit-up at the end is the slow part, so the pose is released a little earlier than other tricks.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** steel freestyle ramp, 38° at the lip, lip 0.5 m high, landing on a dirt mound 0.8 m high with a 31° down slope. Bike speed 3 m/s. Gravity is scaled to 2.6 m/s² so a small ramp gives real-looking hang time.
- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, leaves the lip at 1.46, in the air for 1.79, lands at 3.25, brakes from 4.04, stopped at 5.08. That is 122 keyed frames at 24 a second, 62 sprite frames at 12 a second.
- **Height:** the centre of bike and rider peaks about 2.4 m above the ground.
- **Bike in the air:** 38° nose up at the lip, held for the first 0.27 s, then eases over to 27° nose down just before landing. No roll, no yaw, bars straight. Rear wheel spins 15% faster than on the ground, front wheel slows to half. Suspension hangs out in the air (30 mm rear, 45 mm fork), compresses on the ramp face and on landing, with one small overshoot after the hit.
- **Rider on the ground:** seated to pull away, up into the attack position by 0.7 s, legs soak the ramp, a small pop at the lip, legs soak the landing, sits back to rest while braking.
- **Trick timing after take-off:** starts moving at 0.25 s, fully in the pose at 0.72 s, held until 1.04 s, back in the riding position by 1.50 s. That leaves 0.29 s on the bike before the wheels touch.
- **Pose in the code:** hips 10 cm forward and 10 cm up, body tipped 92° back so it lies flat, feet together 0.95 m ahead of the pegs at 0.86 m high, hands stretched 1.3 m behind at 1.12 m high.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Lying flat along the top of the bike, boots past the bars at the front, arms out past the rear mudguard.

## What to change for the 3D version

Matches well. In v1 the feet sit above bar height in front of the bars; in the real trick the shins are under the handlebar. Check where the legs pass on the 3D bike.

## Best views

Side-on is the best view. A high three-quarter from above shows the body lying along the bike.
