# RideOn

**Kind:** Riding

## What it is

Arriving and stopping. In version 2 these three animations become free riding. The poses below are the building blocks: they should blend with speed, throttle, brake and steering instead of playing as fixed clips.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | Standing, rolls off the throttle. | Engine braking, fork settles slightly. |
| 2 | Braking | Hips back, arms braced, knees gripping. | Fork dives, rear goes light. |
| 3 | Last metre | Sits down in the middle of the seat, one foot comes off its peg and reaches for the ground. | Slows to walking pace, fork starts to come back up. |
| 4 | Stop | Foot plants, body settles, shoulders drop. | Fork rebounds once as the brake is released, bike leans slightly onto the planted foot. |

Small riders on tall bikes stop on tiptoe on one side, with the bike leaning toward that foot.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. From the original base animation set.
- **Length:** 16 sprite frames, 1.3 s. It is the exit from RidingLoop.
- The jump and trick animations contain their own stop: braking over 0.8 s with 40 mm of fork dive, the rider sitting back down to the rest pose during the second half.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Seated rider straightens up as the wheels slow and stop. Both feet stay on the pegs.

## What to change for the 3D version

As with RideOff, no foot goes down. In 3D the stop should end in whatever resting stance is chosen for Idle.

## Best views

Side-on shows the fork dive. Front three-quarter shows the foot going down.
