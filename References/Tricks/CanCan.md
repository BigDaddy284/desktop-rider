# CanCan

**Kind:** Freestyle trick

## What it is

One leg is lifted over the seat to the other side of the bike, so both legs are on the same side, and kicked out. Named after the dance.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lip to 15% | Standing, weight moves onto the foot that stays on its peg. | Nose up. Often leaned a few degrees away from the kicking side. |
| 2 | 15% to 35% | The other foot leaves its peg, the knee lifts and the leg passes over the seat between the rider and the tank. | Level. |
| 3 | 35% to 60% | The leg is straightened out to the side and slightly forward, beside the leg that is still on the peg. Hips turned toward that side. | Level. |
| 4 | 60% to 85% | Knee bends, leg comes back over the seat, boot onto its peg. | Nose drops. |
| 5 | Landing | Standing. A stylish version lands side-saddle. | Rear slightly first. |

A no-footed can can takes the standing foot off as well, so both legs kick out to one side.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** steel freestyle ramp, 38° at the lip, lip 0.5 m high, landing on a dirt mound 0.8 m high with a 31° down slope. Bike speed 3 m/s. Gravity is scaled to 2.6 m/s² so a small ramp gives real-looking hang time.
- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, leaves the lip at 1.46, in the air for 1.79, lands at 3.25, brakes from 4.04, stopped at 5.08. That is 122 keyed frames at 24 a second, 62 sprite frames at 12 a second.
- **Height:** the centre of bike and rider peaks about 2.4 m above the ground.
- **Bike in the air:** 38° nose up at the lip, held for the first 0.27 s, then eases over to 27° nose down just before landing. No roll, no yaw, bars straight. Rear wheel spins 15% faster than on the ground, front wheel slows to half. Suspension hangs out in the air (30 mm rear, 45 mm fork), compresses on the ramp face and on landing, with one small overshoot after the hit.
- **Rider on the ground:** seated to pull away, up into the attack position by 0.7 s, legs soak the ramp, a small pop at the lip, legs soak the landing, sits back to rest while braking.
- **Trick timing after take-off:** starts moving at 0.25 s, fully in the pose at 0.68 s, held until 1.07 s, back in the riding position by 1.50 s. That leaves 0.29 s on the bike before the wheels touch.
- **Pose in the code:** the foot nearest the camera stays on its peg. The far foot comes over to 0.55 m out on the camera's side, 0.62 m ahead of the pegs and 0.62 m high. Hips tilt 14° and the head 10°.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Standing on the near peg with the far leg kicked out toward the viewer in front of the near leg.

## What to change for the 3D version

Matches. v1 always kicks toward the camera, which will not work with a free camera. Pick one side for 3D (for example the right leg always crosses to the left) and keep it.

## Best views

Front three-quarter from the side the leg kicks to. From the other side the leg is hidden behind the bike.
