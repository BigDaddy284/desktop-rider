# Jump

**Kind:** Motocross move

## What it is

A plain racing jump off a dirt take-off. No trick: the aim is to stay balanced, fly level and land on the down slope with the throttle on.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | Standing in the attack position: knees bent and gripping, back flat, elbows up, head over the bars. | Level. Steady throttle, never shut off on the face. |
| 2 | Up the face | Legs and arms soak up the curve, then extend as the bike reaches the lip. | Both ends compress into the face, then rebound at the lip. |
| 3 | Lip | Centred. Too far back and the nose climbs, too far forward and it drops. | Leaves at the angle of the take-off. Suspension tops out. |
| 4 | In the air | Still standing, knees gripping, quiet. A tap of the rear brake drops the nose. A burst of throttle lifts it. | Rotates slowly forward to match the landing. Wheels keep spinning. |
| 5 | Before landing | Looks down the landing, opens the throttle. | Nose slightly down for a down-slope landing, or rear slightly low on a flat landing. |
| 6 | Landing | Legs and arms absorb, hips back a little, throttle on. | Both ends go deep and come back once. The spinning rear wheel drives out of it. |

Brake and throttle in the air work by reaction: stopping the rear wheel tips the nose down, speeding it up lifts the nose. The sharper the change, the stronger the effect. With the clutch pulled in, revving does nothing.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** dirt take-off 30° at the lip, 0.5 m high. Landing mound 0.8 m high, 26° down slope. Speed 3 m/s, gravity scaled to 2.6 m/s².
- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, leaves the lip at 1.62, in the air for 1.33, lands at 2.96, brakes from 3.83, stopped at 4.83. 59 sprite frames.
- **Height:** centre of bike and rider peaks about 1.87 m above the ground.
- **Bike in the air:** 30° nose up at the lip, held 0.2 s, eases to 22° nose down by touchdown. No roll or yaw. Rear wheel spins 15% fast, front slows to half.
- **Rider:** attack position, a little taller and more upright in the air (hips 7 cm up, chest 24° forward). The head stays level while the bike pitches under it.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Standing rider on a bike that floats in an arc from nose up to nose down and lands on the back of the mound.

## What to change for the 3D version

Good match. The rider is very still; real riders visibly extend at the lip and re-bend before landing. A short blip of throttle before touchdown (rear wheel speeding up) would add life.

## Best views

Side-on shows the arc and bike attitude. Front three-quarter from the landing shows the rider's stance.
