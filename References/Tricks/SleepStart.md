# SleepStart

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

The rider dozes off. Leads into Sleeping. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 0.4 s | Shoulders drop, head nods forward once and catches. | Still. |
| 2 | 0.4 to 1.1 s | Folds forward at the waist until the chest and helmet rest on the handlebar pad, arms slack. | Fork compresses slightly as the weight comes onto the bars. |

Tired riders in the pits do rest exactly like this, slumped over the bar pad with the forearms on the bars.
