# Wheelie

**Kind:** Ground trick

## What it is

The front wheel is lifted with throttle and body weight and carried in the air on the rear wheel, then set down.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set-up | Seated toward the back of the seat, arms straight, foot covering the rear brake. | Rolling slowly. |
| 2 | Preload | Pushes down briefly through the bars. | Fork compresses. |
| 3 | Lift | As the fork springs back, opens the throttle sharply and leans back. | Rear squats, fork extends fully, front wheel leaves the ground. |
| 4 | Carry | Arms straight, body leaning back to balance the bike, core rigid. Small throttle changes raise it, a touch of rear brake lowers it. | Front up at 30° to 45° for a short wheelie. The true balance point is much steeper, close to vertical. Front wheel slows down while in the air. |
| 5 | Set down | Rolls the throttle off or dabs the rear brake, weight comes forward. | Front drops, tyre lands, fork compresses and rebounds once. |

The rider's bike is a KTM 50 SX, which has an automatic clutch and one gear. So there is no clutch pop: the lift is throttle and body only.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. It comes from the original base animation set, and only the finished sheet is available here.
- **Length:** 31 sprite frames at 12 a second, 2.6 s, played on the spot.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Seated rider leaning back with arms straight, front wheel lifted to roughly 30°, held for about a second and set down.

## What to change for the 3D version

Looks right for a short power wheelie. In 3D add the fork preload before the lift, the rear squat during it, the front wheel slowing in the air and a fork bounce on set-down, and let it travel forward instead of staying on the spot.
