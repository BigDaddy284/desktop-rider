# Idle

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

The resting loop: the rider sits on the stationary bike. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resting | Seated in the middle of the seat, back relaxed and slightly rounded, arms loose, hands resting on the grips. One foot on the ground, the other on its peg. A small rider reaches the ground on tiptoe. | Leaning a few degrees toward the foot that is down. Front wheel often turned slightly. Engine ticking over, so the bike shivers very slightly. |
| 2 | Breathing | Chest and shoulders rise and fall slowly, about once every 3 to 4 seconds. | Still. |
| 3 | Small movements | Shifts weight on the seat, flexes fingers on the grips, tips the head. | Rocks slightly with the rider. |

A motocross bike has no side stand and cannot stay upright by itself. Either the rider holds it up with a foot or it sits on a separate stand under the frame, as in the owner's photographs of the bike.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. From the original base animation set.
- **Length:** 24 sprite frames, a 2.0 s loop.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Rider sits upright with both feet on the pegs and both hands on the bars. The bike stands perfectly upright by itself. There is a gentle breathing movement.

## What to change for the 3D version

The balancing bike was acceptable in a flat side view but will look odd once it can be seen from the front or back. This needs a decision: one foot down with the bike leaning, or keep the cartoon upright balance. One of the owner's photographs shows the rider seated at a start gate, which is the best guide to the natural resting stance.

## Best views

Front three-quarter shows the lean and the foot on the ground. Side-on shows the seated posture.

## This rider's own style

The owner's start-gate video shows the rider resting on the stationary bike seated forward with **both feet on the ground**, elbows up. That supports a feet-down resting pose over the version 1 balancing bike. See [RIDER_STYLE.md](RIDER_STYLE.md).
