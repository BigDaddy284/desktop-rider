# Revving

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

The rider blips the throttle while stationary. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blip | Right wrist rolls back sharply and returns. Fingers of the right hand stay over the front brake lever. Forearm tenses. | Engine note rises. The whole bike shakes, most visibly at the mudguards and bar ends. A puff of blue two-stroke smoke from the silencer on the right side. |
| 2 | Between blips | Wrist relaxed. | Settles back to tick-over. |

The rider's bike is a 50cc two-stroke with an automatic clutch. If it is revved hard while standing still the clutch starts to bite and the bike tries to creep forward, so the rider holds the brake. The rear end squats slightly each time.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. From the original base animation set.
- **Length:** 12 sprite frames, a 1.0 s loop.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

The bike and rider shake quickly on the spot. Rider seated, leaning very slightly forward.

## What to change for the 3D version

For 3D add the wrist roll, exhaust smoke on the right side where the silencer is, and a slight rear squat with each blip. The shake should be strongest in the thin plastic parts, not a rigid shake of everything.

## Best views

Rear three-quarter on the right side shows the wrist and the exhaust together.
