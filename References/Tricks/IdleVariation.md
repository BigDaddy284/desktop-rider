# IdleVariation

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

An occasional fidget that breaks up the Idle loop: the rider looks around and shifts weight. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 1 s | Head turns to one side, shoulders follow a little. | Still. |
| 2 | 1 to 2 s | Looks the other way, or down at the bike. | Still. |
| 3 | 2 to 3 s | Shifts on the seat, stretches the arms or rolls the shoulders, may take one hand off to shake it out. | Rocks slightly, fork moves a few millimetres. |
| 4 | 3 to 4 s | Settles back to the resting pose. | Still. |

Riders waiting on a start line fidget constantly: adjusting goggles, pulling at gloves, wiping the lens, blipping the throttle, looking at the riders either side.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. From the original base animation set.
- **Length:** 49 sprite frames, 4.1 s, played once at random intervals.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

The head turns toward the viewer, then away, with a small weight shift. Feet stay on the pegs.

## What to change for the 3D version

Fine as it is. For 3D, a few different fidgets chosen at random (goggle adjust, glove tug, look down at the bike) would stop it repeating.

## Best views

Front three-quarter, because most of the movement is in the head and shoulders.
