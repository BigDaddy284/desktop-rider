# Whoops

**Kind:** Supercross move

## What it is

A row of evenly spaced bumps. Taken fast ('blitzing' or 'skimming'), the wheels touch only the tops, the suspension does the work and the bike stays almost level while the rider stands with the weight back.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Entry | Standing, already at the speed to be held all the way through. One gear higher than seems natural. | Level, front wheel placed on the upper part of the first whoop, not at the top and not in the trough. |
| 2 | First two whoops | Hips back, knees behind the toes, head still over the bars. Balls of the feet on the pegs, legs squeezing the bike. | Front light and skimming. Rear driving off the back face of each whoop. |
| 3 | Through the middle | Upper body square and up, elbows high, arms and shoulders loose and following. Legs working like extra suspension so the head stays level. | Wheels patter from top to top. Fork and shock moving quickly through a lot of travel. Small pitch changes only. |
| 4 | When it goes wrong | Thrown forward. | Front drops into a trough or the rear kicks up and sideways (a swap). |
| 5 | Exit | Weight comes forward again, sits or brakes for the corner. | Rear settles. |

Speed makes them easier. Entering slowly and accelerating in the middle loses grip. Leaning too far back makes the front miss whoops altogether. The alternative to skimming is jumping them two or three at a time, used when they are worn down.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** seven whoops, 1.3 m apart and 0.30 m tall. The bike's wheelbase is 1.09 m.
- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, rides at 3.3 m/s (10% faster than normal riding), one whoop every 0.39 s, brakes from 4.0, stopped at 5.0. 61 sprite frames.
- **Bike:** the wheels ride a path that fills the troughs to 58% of the whoop height, so they skim. Pitch still swings about 16° each way. The chassis follows a smoothed path and the difference shows as suspension movement (rear up to 90 mm, fork up to 50 mm). Rear wheel spins 12% faster than ground speed.
- **Rider:** standing, hips 7 cm back and 11 cm up, chest about 26° forward, bobbing against the bike to hold the head level.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Standing rider, weight back, bike rocking gently over a row of rounded bumps with both wheels close to the tops.

## What to change for the 3D version

Believable. The 16° pitch swing is on the large side for a clean blitz, where the bike looks almost level; that amount suits a slower rider. Real supercross whoops are steeper and closer together relative to the bike than v1's rounded ones.

## Best views

Side-on is by far the best view and is how they are normally filmed. Front three-quarter from the exit shows elbows and knees.
