# Thinking

**Kind:** Character

## What it is

Shown while the AI is working on a reply. A character animation, not a riding technique, so there is no single real-world reference. The notes describe how a real rider behaves in the same situation so the 3D version can borrow believable detail.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 0.6 s | One hand leaves the grip and comes up to the chin bar of the helmet. | Still. |
| 2 | 0.6 to 2.4 s | Hand rests on the chin bar, head tilted and turned slightly, as if pondering. Small nods or a finger tap. | Still. |
| 3 | 2.4 to 3.1 s | Hand returns to the grip, head straightens. | Still. |

With a full-face helmet the hand cannot reach the chin, so it rests on the chin bar or taps the side of the helmet. Motocross gloves are bulky, so the gesture is with the whole hand.
