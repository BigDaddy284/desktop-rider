# RidingLoop

**Kind:** Riding

## What it is

Steady riding. In version 2 these three animations become free riding. The poses below are the building blocks: they should blend with speed, throttle, brake and steering instead of playing as fixed clips.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standing (the default) | Balls of the feet on the pegs, knees slightly bent and gripping the bike, hips over the pegs, back flat and tipped forward, elbows up and out, head over the bars looking far ahead. Riders stand for most of a lap. | Free to move underneath. Suspension working over bumps while the rider's head stays steady. |
| 2 | Seated | Used for corners and when maximum grip is needed. Sits at the very front of the seat against the tank, back straight, elbows up. | More weight on the front tyre. |
| 3 | Corner entry | Braking is finished while still standing. Then sits forward and puts the inside leg out: straight ahead beside the front wheel, toe pointed in, knee slightly bent, foot just clear of the ground. Outside elbow high. | Leaned into the corner. The rider's upper body stays more upright than the bike, sitting on the outside edge of the seat. |
| 4 | Mid corner | Weight pressed through the outside peg, outside knee against the tank. Head turned to look at the exit. | Leaned 30° to 45° in a berm or rut, less on flat ground. Front wheel steered into the turn. Rear may drift out slightly under power. |
| 5 | Corner exit | Rolls the throttle on, inside foot comes back to its peg, body moves back to centre and stands up. | Stands up out of the lean. Rear squats and throws dirt (roost). |

The key look of motocross cornering is that the bike leans more than the rider. That is the opposite of road racing, where the rider hangs inside.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

Not in the trick scripts. From the original base animation set.
- **Length:** 12 sprite frames, a 1.0 s loop, played on the spot while the application moves the window at a steady speed.
- **Speed used elsewhere:** 3 m/s in the miniature world, with the wheels turning to match.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Seated, upright, both feet on the pegs, wheels turning. No standing, no lean, no suspension movement.

## What to change for the 3D version

v1 only has one seated pose. Free riding needs at least: standing, seated, lean left and right with the inside foot out, accelerating and braking. The owner's reference photographs include one corner and one jump for the rider's own style (see PHOTO_AUDIT.md).

## Best views

Front three-quarter from the outside of a corner shows the lean, the foot out and the elbows. Side-on shows the standing position best.

## This rider's own style

From the owner's photographs (see [RIDER_STYLE.md](RIDER_STYLE.md)). These take priority over the general technique above.

- **Seated is the default**, not standing: chest forward and low over the bar pad, elbows out wide and up. Stands only for jumps and bumps.
- **Corners:** sits at the very front of the seat, inside leg straight out ahead with the boot level with the front axle and toe up, outside elbow high, head turned to the exit. The bike leans 30° to 45° while the body stays nearer upright.
