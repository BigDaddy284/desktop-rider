# HeelClicker

**Kind:** Freestyle trick

## What it is

Both legs come up and around the outside of the arms and the boot heels are clicked together above the front of the bike, in front of the bars.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lip to 15% | Standing, then jumps the feet up off the pegs. | Nose up. |
| 2 | 15% to 35% | Knees come up outside the elbows, the legs wrap over the arms and extend forward over the bars. The rider sits back to balance. | Front dips a little as the legs go forward. |
| 3 | 35% to 55% | Heels meet above the front mudguard or number plate. One clean click, held for a moment. Arms nearly straight, body leaning back. | Level. |
| 4 | 55% to 85% | Legs open, swing back around the arms and down to the pegs. | Nose drops. |
| 5 | Landing | Standing. | Rear slightly first. |

The legs must go outside the arms. If they pass between the arms it is a different trick (a bar hop).

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** steel freestyle ramp, 38° at the lip, lip 0.5 m high, landing on a dirt mound 0.8 m high with a 31° down slope. Bike speed 3 m/s. Gravity is scaled to 2.6 m/s² so a small ramp gives real-looking hang time.
- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, leaves the lip at 1.46, in the air for 1.79, lands at 3.25, brakes from 4.04, stopped at 5.08. That is 122 keyed frames at 24 a second, 62 sprite frames at 12 a second.
- **Height:** the centre of bike and rider peaks about 2.4 m above the ground.
- **Bike in the air:** 38° nose up at the lip, held for the first 0.27 s, then eases over to 27° nose down just before landing. No roll, no yaw, bars straight. Rear wheel spins 15% faster than on the ground, front wheel slows to half. Suspension hangs out in the air (30 mm rear, 45 mm fork), compresses on the ramp face and on landing, with one small overshoot after the hit.
- **Rider on the ground:** seated to pull away, up into the attack position by 0.7 s, legs soak the ramp, a small pop at the lip, legs soak the landing, sits back to rest while braking.
- **Trick timing after take-off:** starts moving at 0.25 s, fully in the pose at 0.68 s, held until 1.07 s, back in the riding position by 1.50 s. That leaves 0.29 s on the bike before the wheels touch.
- **Pose in the code:** hips 12 cm back, leaning back at the hips and forward at the chest, both feet 0.8 m ahead of the pegs at 1.2 m high and 7 cm either side of the centre line. The feet tap in and out by 3 cm about five times.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Sitting back with both legs lifted straight out over the bars and the boots together above the front number plate.

## What to change for the 3D version

Pose is right. Real riders click once or twice, not five times. In 3D make sure each leg passes outside its arm on the way out and back; v1 never had to solve that because it was hidden side-on.

## Best views

Front three-quarter shows the legs wrapped around the arms and the heels meeting. Side-on shows the height.
