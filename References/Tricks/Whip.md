# Whip

**Kind:** Motocross move

## What it is

The bike is laid over sideways in the air with the back end swung out, then brought back straight for the landing. Pure style. A flat whip gets the bike close to horizontal.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approach | Standing. Slightly faster than for a straight jump. Picks a side. | Level. |
| 2 | Up the face | Carves a gentle arc across the face, about 300 to 500 mm off the straight line from bottom to top, weight slightly to the inside. Steady throttle. | Leaning into the arc, tyres gripping. This arc is what starts the whip. |
| 3 | Lip | As the front leaves, weight comes back to the centre and the bars are turned the opposite way (counter-steer). Throttle shuts as the rear leaves. | Front goes one way, the rear follows the arc and swings out and up around the steering head. |
| 4 | Peak (about 40% to 50%) | Body moves up onto the high side of the bike. Low foot firmly on its peg, the inside hand barely holding. The movement comes from the torso and hips. | Laid over up to 90°, pointing across the direction of travel, front wheel turned back toward the landing. |
| 5 | Bringing it back | Past the top, opens the throttle hard and holds it. | The spinning rear wheel pulls the back end down and into line. |
| 6 | Landing | Both feet on, both hands firm, centred. | Rear wheel first. Rarely perfectly straight, even when it looks it from the side. |

The flight path is fixed at take-off. In the air the rider can only adjust attitude. Shutting the throttle frees the rear to swing, opening it brings the rear back.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** dirt take-off 32°, lip 0.55 m. Landing mound 0.85 m, 27° down slope.
- **Timeline (seconds):** leaves the lip at 1.62, in the air for 1.46, lands at 3.08, brakes from 3.96, stopped at 5.00. 61 sprite frames. Peak height 2.01 m.
- **Whip timing after take-off:** starts at 0.15 s, full at 0.61 s, held until 0.76 s, straight again by 1.28 s, which is 0.18 s before touchdown.
- **Bike at full whip:** rolled 62° toward the camera, yawed 30°, bars turned 22°. Pitch goes from 32° nose up to 23° nose down as in a normal jump.
- **Rider:** hips shifted 4 cm, chest leaning 14° and head 22° to stay more upright than the bike.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Bike laid over toward the viewer in the air showing its top side and the number on the rider's back, then straightened for the landing.

## What to change for the 3D version

The air part is good. Missing: the arc up the face (v1 rides straight up and the roll starts only after the lip) and the throttle story (shut at the lip, hard on to bring it back, so the rear wheel should slow and then speed up). v1 always whips toward the camera; in 3D pick a direction per jump.

## Best views

Rear three-quarter shows how far the back end swings. Front three-quarter from the landing shows how flat the bike gets. Side-on hides most of it.
