# Superman

**Kind:** Freestyle trick

## What it is

Both feet leave the pegs and the legs are thrown straight back, so the rider lies flat in the air behind the bars, holding on with both hands. The base of the whole superman family.

## The real move: key poses in order

| # | When | Rider | Bike |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On the ramp | Standing, weight centred, arms nearly straight, squeezing the bike with the knees. Steady throttle. | Suspension compressed into the curve of the ramp. |
| 2 | Lip to 15% of the flight | Neutral, lets the bike settle. Eyes on the landing. | Leaves nose up at the ramp angle. Suspension tops out. |
| 3 | 15% to 35% | Lets go with the knees, pushes the hips up and back, kicks both legs straight back together. Arms straighten as the body goes out. | Pushing the body back pulls the bars: the front comes up a little and the bike moves forward under the rider. |
| 4 | 35% to 60% (the peak) | Body flat or slightly above flat, legs together and straight, toes pointed, head up looking at the landing. Hands on the grips. | Roughly level, floating. This is the frame people photograph. |
| 5 | 60% to 85% | Pulls with the arms and bends at the waist, knees come forward, feet find the pegs. Sits or half stands. | Nose starts to drop to match the landing. |
| 6 | Landing | Standing, knees and elbows bent, throttle on. | Rear wheel a touch before the front on the down slope. Both ends compress deeply and settle. |

Arms stay almost straight at the peak. The rider's hips are above seat height, not trailing below it. The further the body goes back, the more the bike's nose rises, so riders leave the ramp with the front slightly low to make room for it.

## Version 1, for comparison

**Timing and bike motion**

- **Stage:** steel freestyle ramp, 38° at the lip, lip 0.5 m high, landing on a dirt mound 0.8 m high with a 31° down slope. Bike speed 3 m/s. Gravity is scaled to 2.6 m/s² so a small ramp gives real-looking hang time.
- **Timeline (seconds):** pulls away 0 to 0.6, leaves the lip at 1.46, in the air for 1.79, lands at 3.25, brakes from 4.04, stopped at 5.08. That is 122 keyed frames at 24 a second, 62 sprite frames at 12 a second.
- **Height:** the centre of bike and rider peaks about 2.4 m above the ground.
- **Bike in the air:** 38° nose up at the lip, held for the first 0.27 s, then eases over to 27° nose down just before landing. No roll, no yaw, bars straight. Rear wheel spins 15% faster than on the ground, front wheel slows to half. Suspension hangs out in the air (30 mm rear, 45 mm fork), compresses on the ramp face and on landing, with one small overshoot after the hit.
- **Rider on the ground:** seated to pull away, up into the attack position by 0.7 s, legs soak the ramp, a small pop at the lip, legs soak the landing, sits back to rest while braking.
- **Trick timing after take-off:** starts moving at 0.25 s, fully in the pose at 0.68 s, held until 1.07 s, back in the riding position by 1.50 s. That leaves 0.29 s on the bike before the wheels touch.
- **Pose in the code:** hips 0.32 m back and 0.37 m up, body pitched 75° forward so it lies flat, head lifted 58° to look ahead, both feet together 1.6 m behind the pegs line at seat-top height, hands stay on the grips.

**Pose in the version 1 sprite sheet**

Body flat and straight behind the bike at about bar height, legs together, boots trailing past the rear mudguard, both hands on the bars. Reads clearly from the side.

## What to change for the 3D version

Close to the real thing. The bike does not react when the rider throws the legs back: add a few degrees of nose-up as the body goes out and the same back as the rider returns. Entry and exit are a straight blend, where the real move bends at the waist first and the legs follow.

## Best views

Side-on shows the flat body line best. A front three-quarter from the landing side shows the arms and the head looking forward. From behind, check the legs stay together.
