## Colour scheme

The whole look is built on four colours plus the bike's factory orange:

- **Hot pink / magenta**, the dominant accent on both bike and rider.
- **Sky blue**.
- **White**, used for the number backgrounds and rear bodywork.
- **Black**, used for the numbers, seat and graphic breaks.
- **Orange**, the bike's own plastics, frame parts and swingarm showing around the graphics.

## Bike

A small-wheel KTM youth motocross bike (KTM branding is visible on the grips and silencer). The exact model and year are not confirmed.

**Shared details**

- Race number **28** in bold black block numerals with a white inline, on a white background, on the front plate and both side panels.
- The rider's name appears above the number on the front plate and beneath the number on each side panel, in blue capitals on a pink band.
- Black seat.
- Black wheel rims, silver spokes, gold anodised hubs, knobbly tyres.

- Blue anodised footpegs.

- White rear fender and side panels with pink and blue stripes and a cluster of small sponsor decals.

## Rider

A young rider in full motocross gear.

- **Helmet:** Full-face motocross helmet with peak. Busy multi-colour graphic of sky blue, magenta, fluorescent yellow and black in jagged, radiating shapes, with a pink lower edge trim and a pink and blue chin bar with a yellow-green mouth vent.
- **Goggles:** Black frame with pink and yellow splatter pattern; pink strap with a black repeating pattern; clear lens.
- **Jersey:** Sky blue base with magenta and black diagonal brush-stroke bands and thin white zig-zag line work. V-neck with blue collar. A large white brand logo sits on the chest. On the back, the rider's name in pink capitals above a large pink **28** with a black outline.
- **Trousers:** Matching sky blue, magenta and black pattern, with a black seat panel and black knee areas.
- **Gloves:** Black with white detailing.
- **Boots:** White, tall motocross boots with black logo patches on the shins.

## Not yet known

These are needed before final artwork and must not be guessed:

1. **The rider sitting on the bike**, ideally from the side. Needed for proportions and the base riding pose that every animation builds on.
2. **The rear of the bike.** Needed for ride-off animations and the rear fender design.
3. **The sides of the helmet.** Only the front and back are visible; the side graphics are what show most in a side-on character.
4. **The side of the boots and the gloves close up.** Needed because the character is mostly seen from the side.
5. **The bike's model and year.**
