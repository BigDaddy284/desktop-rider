### Proportions-only photographs

The owner supplied five action photographs **for proportions and riding poses only**. Several show a different kit, a different helmet or older bike graphics. Nothing about colour, graphics or equipment may be taken from them:

- Side-on, seated on the bike (older kit and older bike graphics).
- Professional cornering photograph (older kit; third-party copyright, reference only).
- Mid-air over a jump and leaning through a corner (different kit and helmet).
- Standing beside the bike, holding the handlebars (different kit and helmet).

What they establish:

- Seated, the rider's knees are bent to roughly a right angle with the boots on the footpegs, and the top of the helmet is roughly one and a half times the height of the handlebars from the ground.
- The rider sits well forward, close to the tank, with elbows raised and out.
- Standing beside the bike, the handlebars come to about the rider's chest and the seat to about the hip.
- The helmet is large in relation to the body, as it is on any young rider, which suits slightly exaggerated cartoon proportions without invention.

## Colour scheme

The whole look is built on four colours plus the bike's factory orange:

- **Hot pink / magenta**, the dominant accent on both bike and rider.
- **Sky blue**.
- **White**, used for the number backgrounds and rear bodywork.
- **Black**, used for the numbers, seat and graphic breaks.
- **Orange**, the bike's own plastics, frame parts and swingarm showing around the graphics.

## Bike

A **KTM 50 SX, 2013 model**, confirmed by the owner, with these modifications:

- Big wheel kit and extended swingarm kit.
- Full FMF exhaust: chrome expansion pipe and silencer.
- VHM cylinder head (gold anodised, visible above the cylinder on the left side).
- RFX footpegs (blue anodised, wide, with steel teeth).
- Talon hubs, custom painted, with Excel rims.

## Rider

A young rider in full motocross gear.

- **Helmet:** (front, back and left side available) Full-face motocross helmet with peak. Busy multi-colour graphic of sky blue, magenta, fluorescent yellow and black in jagged, radiating shapes, with a pink lower edge trim and a pink and blue chin bar with a yellow-green mouth vent. From the left side: the shell is mainly sky blue; a large fluorescent yellow panel sits above the eye opening; a magenta stripe runs along the top of the peak and crown; bold black angular lettering-style shapes with blue infill cover the rear half; yellow and black bands with a small animal-head logo sit low at the back; the chin bar has fine yellow rays fanning forward over magenta; a magenta band runs around the bottom edge; black liner and blue cheek pads show in the opening.
- **Goggles:** Black frame with pink and yellow splatter pattern; pink strap with a black repeating pattern; clear lens.
- **Jersey:** Sky blue base with magenta and black diagonal brush-stroke bands, halftone dot shading where colours meet, and thin white zig-zag line work. Blue V-neck at the front, black collar band at the back. A large white brand logo sits on the chest.
  - **Back:** the rider's first name in small capitals above the surname in large capitals, both in a collegiate block typeface, pink with a darker outline. Beneath, a large pink **28** in the same block style with a black outline and drop shadow. Below the number is the team logo, then two smaller logos.
  - **Sleeves:** a large brand logo at the top of each sleeve, then a column of small sponsor patches, then one long sponsor logo running down the forearm.
- **Trousers:** Matching sky blue, magenta and black pattern on the front and sides. From behind: a large black seat and inner-leg panel, plain sky blue down the back of the legs, and the team logo with the number 28 printed just below the waistband. Brand logos on the thighs and shins.
- **Gloves:** Black with white detailing. No close-up available.
- **Boots:** Tall white motocross boots with four white buckle straps on the outer side, a black inner gaiter visible at the front opening, black sole, and fine black stitching lines. Each boot has three custom colour inserts in the kit colours, and the two boots are deliberately opposite to each other:

  | Insert | Left boot | Right boot |
  |---|---|---|
  | Starburst panel on the upper outer side | Sky blue with pink rays | Pink with sky blue rays |
  | Teardrop logo on the outer heel | Pink with a blue centre | Sky blue with a pink centre |
  | Teardrop badge on the front of the shin (black surround) | Sky blue with a pink "1" | Pink with a sky blue "1" |

  Left and right were worked out from which side the buckles are on, and should be confirmed with the owner at concept stage.
